# Q-AICON Lab

### Quantum and AI Computing, Optimization, and Networking

**Intelligence. Quantum. Communication.**

Q-AICON is a research group at **Western University**, London, Ontario, Canada, led by **Dr. Fang (Fiona) Fang**, Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering and the Department of Computer Science.

We study how artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and communication networks can work together to make complex systems more efficient, adaptive, and trustworthy. Our research connects mathematical modeling and algorithm design with experimental validation and application-driven system development.

## Research vision

Our work addresses two complementary challenges: using intelligence to improve communication and computing infrastructure, and designing infrastructure that can support distributed intelligence. Alongside these efforts, we investigate resource-efficient quantum methods for optimization problems in wireless communications and networking.

We aim to develop methods that account for practical constraints, including limited communication bandwidth, computing resources, quantum hardware capacity, and security requirements.

## Research directions

### Artificial intelligence and distributed learning

We develop communication-efficient learning and inference methods for connected systems, with an emphasis on:

- Edge AI and federated learning over wireless networks.
- Semantic and task-oriented communications.
- Digital-twin-assisted learning and resource allocation.
- Coordination between learning algorithms and communication infrastructure.

### Quantum computing and optimization

We investigate quantum approaches to combinatorial optimization, focusing on:

- Qubit-efficient quantum approximate optimization algorithms (QAOA).
- Symmetry-aware formulations and reduced quantum representations.
- Quantum optimization for communication-system design, including pilot assignment.
- The relationship between computational resources and solution quality.

### Wireless communications and trustworthy networking

We study the design and optimization of flexible, secure communication systems, including:

- Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS).
- Pinching-antenna systems and antenna placement.
- Non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA).
- Physical-layer security and trustworthy wireless communications.
- Zero-trust access control for Internet of Things (IoT) systems.

## From research to applications

Our applied projects explore how these methods can support indoor localization, measurement-based mental health care, context-aware smart homes, UAV-assisted wildfire monitoring, smart agriculture, and transportation safety. These projects connect foundational questions with the constraints and needs of real systems.

Explore the [project portfolio](https://fionauwo.github.io/QAICON/projects/) for project descriptions and associated research topics.

## About this repository

This repository hosts the generated files for the Q-AICON Quantum website. It presents the group's research, projects, publications, members, and academic news. Research code and datasets are not included unless explicitly added and documented.

The site is prepared for GitHub Pages at **`/QAICON/`**, with `main` as the publishing branch and the repository root as the publishing directory.

### Website maintenance

The editable website source is maintained separately in the `aicon-concepts` project. To update this site:

1. Build the source with `BASE_PATH=/QAICON/`.
2. Copy the generated `dist` contents into this repository, preserving this README and `.nojekyll`.
3. Check the generated pages and internal links, then commit and push to `main`.

The deployment path must remain `/QAICON/` so that assets and navigation resolve correctly.
