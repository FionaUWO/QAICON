Conventions: maximize ⟨C⟩; UC(γ) = exp(−iγC), UB(β) = exp(−iβΣXᵢ). The leftmost bit is q₀; all angles are in radians. Gate decompositions omit global phase, so amplitudes may have a common phase offset from the block view while measurement probabilities agree.
